# Games

Open GAMES in the [menu](menu.md). Each game keeps your best score, even after you turn the device off, and the menu shows it at the right end of the game's row.

In every game, F1 (SET) opens the RECEIVE settings, F3 (STOP or GO) pauses the game or starts a new one once it ends, and F4 (BACK) returns to the menu. When a game ends, the footer says how it went and `NEW BEST!` when it was your best. Press F3 (GO) or key any character to play again.

INVADERS and SIMON use the characters of CHARS SET, in the RECEIVE section of the [settings](settings.md#receive): A-Z unless you change it.

## LADDER

Callsigns, after the RufzXP contest trainer. The device sends a call; copy it and key it back.

- Right: you score its length times the speed, and the next call comes 2 WPM faster.
- Wrong: no score, and the next call comes 2 WPM slower.

A run is 25 calls. It starts at CHAR SPEED and goes from 10 to 60 WPM. F2 (AGN) sends the call again, but then it scores half. If you don't start keying within 8 seconds, the call counts as missed.

Each row shows the call, your copy marked against it, and what it scored at what speed, such as `+120 24W`.

## INVADERS

Characters fall in eight columns. Key a character to shoot the lowest one like it. Each hit scores 10. Every 100 points is a level: they fall faster and come more often. Three that reach the ground end the game. The header shows your score, level and lives.

## SIMON

The device sends a sequence; key it back. Each time you get it all right, the next one is one character longer. A miss ends the game. Your score is the longest sequence you keyed back, up to 20. F2 (AGN) sends it again before you start keying.

## WORDLE

Guess the five-letter word in six tries, by keying it. After each guess, its letters color:

- Green: the right letter in the right place.
- Amber: in the word, somewhere else.
- Gray: not in the word.

The alphabet under the score shows what you know about each letter. A guess that isn't five letters doesn't count. Your score is the number of words you get in a row.

## FIST

The device shows a word or callsign and its code; key it. The game times every mark and gap you key against perfect timing, as [Timing](timing.md) does, and scores the word as the percent that were good. A word keyed wrong scores 0. A run is 10 words; your score is the run's average.

With paddles, the keyer times the dits and dahs, so the score is about your spacing. With a straight key or a sideswiper, it's about all of it.
